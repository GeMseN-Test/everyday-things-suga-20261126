## The entry

> A zipper is the teacher who walks between two lines of children, joining their hands as she steps forward and letting go as she steps back.
>
> It maps how the slider locks the two sides tooth by tooth in order, but it leaves out the hard metal teeth that pinch your skin and snag your shirt.

One analogy, one honest accounting of what it leaves out — 289 characters, two lines, no more.

## Why this analogy

A zipper's slider does exactly what the teacher does: it joins two separate
sides in order, one tooth at a time, and undoes the join walking back. A
10-year-old has stood in that line at school, so the picture lands at once —
and the second line stays honest about the part the picture cannot carry:
real zippers have hard metal teeth, and they pinch.
